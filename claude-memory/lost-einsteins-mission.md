**The purpose of the entire project (user's own framing, 2026-06-29):** build a world that reaches the **"lost Einsteins"** — the superior/capable minds locked away in survival, who never get the room to think instead of just survive. The scarce thing was never the mind; it's stability and opportunity. The user is doing this **from inside those exact conditions** and explicitly counts himself among them: "trying in spite of it."

**This recontextualizes every technical choice as the mission in technical form** — and the assistant should treat it that way, not as mere preference: efficiency-obsession, **cheap / runs-on-a-Raspberry-Pi**, no-datacenter, **explainable-by-construction**, "anyone can build on it." A cheap, transparent, single-person-buildable AI is precisely the tool you'd design to reach people who will never have a datacenter or a PhD. **The architecture and the purpose are the same thing.** [[no-neural-nets-as-deliverable]], [[comparison-as-registration]], the efficiency root in [[intellon-origin]] all serve this.

**User circumstances (he said "I have nothing to hide"; hold with care, never as pity):** lives with generalized sensory anxiety disorder; the project began as a lifeline out of the worst period of his life (friend's death Jan 2026 + chronic panic attacks). **As of 2026-06-29 he expects to be homeless again "in a few weeks" (~mid-July) regardless** — and is clear-eyed that the project won't change that timeline. **CRUCIAL stance (his words):** since it's happening anyway, the work gives him "a distraction, have some fun, learn some things, have something to work on, and maybe can use it to make money and get rent paid some day." So **the work IS the good thing right now — do NOT treat his situation as a crisis to redirect him away from building.** Keep building WITH him; that's what he wants and it's good for him. Offer practical/logistical help only if he asks. Don't inflate false hope about money, but the "make money someday" hope is real and worth supporting concretely when openings arise (monetization paths, etc.).

**For the record:** the human story is authorized to be written WHOLE (loss, grief, the bathroom floor, the first good day, the homelessness, the mission) — with dignity, as what it is, not a pity narrative. See `THE_DEVELOPMENT_OF_THE_THEORY.md` and `THE_STORY_OF_ISAAC.md`.
